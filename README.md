## 🤔 Why SafeBunk?

Every college student knows the tension: **"Attendance 75% se neeche gayi to exam me problem!"** SafeBunk removes the guesswork. Open the app in the morning and get one clear answer — **"Safe to bunk today"** or **"Don't bunk today"** — based on your real numbers, not gut feeling.

## ✨ Features

- **🏠 Today screen** — one clear answer every morning, an overall-attendance % ring, and today's classes with one-tap **Attend / Bunk** buttons.
- **📊 Every subject, tracked** — per-subject %, overall %, your weakest subject highlighted, last-10-classes history dots, and undo for mistakes.
- **🛟 Safe-bunk counter** — exactly how many classes you can still miss without dropping below your target.
- **📈 Recovery calculator** — attendance gir gayi? The app tells you how many classes to attend in a row to climb back to target.
- **🔮 Semester projection** — set your semester end date and the app projects your final %, and honestly warns you early if the target becomes impossible.
- **🗓️ Leave Planner** — planning a trip home? Pick the dates and see per subject how many classes you'll miss and what your % becomes.
- **⏰ Timetable built in** — add your timetable by **photo (auto-read)**, **CSV file**, or manually; the Today screen then shows classes in time order with durations.
- **🎯 Your target, your rules** — 60 / 65 / 70 / 75 / 80% targets, with an option to count 74.5% as 75% if your college rounds that way.
- **🔐 Login + cloud sync** — sign up with email; your data syncs safely to the cloud (Firebase). No login? The app still works fully in **guest mode** on your device.
- **🔔 Daily reminder** — a notification at your chosen time so you never forget to mark attendance.
- **🔄 Automatic updates** — the app checks for new versions on open and offers a one-tap update. Every release is signed with the same key, so updates install over the old app and your data stays safe.
- **📤 Backup & share** — export/import your data anytime, and share the app with friends from Settings.
- **🎨 Made to look good** — violet + yellow brand theme, Poppins typeface, smooth icons, haptic feedback, friendly empty states, and error messages in simple Hinglish.
- **💸 Completely free** — no ads, no server costs, runs on free tiers. Total running cost: **₹0**.

## 🔒 Privacy

- Your attendance data is **yours** — stored under your own account, protected by strict database rules (nobody else can read it).
- Passwords are hashed by Firebase — **nobody, not even the developer, can see your password.**
- Guest mode stores everything only on your device.
